BDNet Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

BDNet is an iPhone app that connects to BDNet aerosol sensors over Bluetooth and displays their readings. It is developed for S3i and distributed through Apple TestFlight by OmniTech Ventures LLC. This policy describes what the app does with information.

Information the app collects

BDNet does not collect, store, or transmit personal information. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Bluetooth

The app asks for Bluetooth permission so it can find and connect to nearby BDNet sensors. Bluetooth is used only to receive readings from those sensors and to request files from them. The app does not use Bluetooth to track your location or to identify you.

Sensor data

Readings received from a sensor are shown in the app and kept in the phone's memory for up to one hour so charts can be displayed. They are not sent to the developer or to any other party. Files you choose to download from a sensor are saved on your phone, in the app's own folder, where you can view, share, or delete them through the Files app. Removing the app removes them.

Information sent to Apple

When you use BDNet through TestFlight, Apple may collect crash logs and usage statistics under Apple's TestFlight terms, and you can share feedback with the developer through TestFlight. That information is handled according to Apple's privacy policy.

Changes

If the app begins handling information differently, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the feedback address listed for BDNet in TestFlight.
